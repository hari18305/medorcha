# Information and consent sheet

**Study:** How clearly does a clinical decision-support prototype explain its results?
**Team:** B.Tech CSE project team, Amrita School of Computing, Amritapuri. **Guide:** Dr. Sunitha E V.

## What this is
We are testing a research prototype called MedOrchestrate. You enter a short clinical case (made up, never a real patient), and the
system proposes a ranked list of possible diagnoses with its evidence, and says whether a clinician should review the result.
We want to find out where the screens are confusing so that we can improve them. **We are testing the software, not you.**
There are no right or wrong impressions.

## What you will do (about 35 to 40 minutes)
- Work through a few made-up cases on two versions of the screen, while saying out loud what you are thinking.
- Answer a few short questions about what the screen showed.
- Fill in a short questionnaire and give feedback.

## Important
- **The prototype is not a medical device** and must not be used for real patients. Its results are not clinical advice.
- All cases are made up or use openly licensed images. Please do not type any real patient information.
- Taking part is **voluntary**. You may skip any question and stop at any time, without giving a reason.

## Your information
- You will be identified only as a number (for example P3). We record your role (for example "junior doctor") and years of experience, but not your name.
- We take handwritten notes. **Screen or audio recording happens only if you agree below.**
- Anonymous results (scores and short comments) may appear in our project report and presentation.
